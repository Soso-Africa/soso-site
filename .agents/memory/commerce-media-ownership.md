---
name: Commerce media ownership
description: Distinguishes SOSO editorial imagery from JusticeSure inventory and payment authority.
---

SOSO storefront images come from Staff-approved, published product media. JusticeSure supplies authoritative product identity, variants, availability, and money; a lack of provider-hosted media is not a reason to reject an otherwise verified mapping.

**Why:** The merchant explicitly keeps product photos in SOSO's Staff portal. Requiring duplicate uploads in JusticeSure would block a valid purchase without improving payment integrity.

**How to apply:** Validate SOSO's published media and its provenance independently, then match the product and each eligible variant to the JusticeSure catalogue with exact price and stock checks. Do not substitute a JusticeSure image or fabricate an editorial photo when the SOSO item is missing approved media.

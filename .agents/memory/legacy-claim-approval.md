---
name: Legacy claim approval
description: Editorial evidence and indexing rules for preserved legacy business content.
---

Preserving first-party legacy copy establishes provenance but does not substantiate statistics, forecasts, programme outcomes, partnerships, or future commercial commitments. Without separate approved evidence, remove the claim or qualify it as attributed history, ambition, availability-dependent service, or non-binding enquiry.

**Why:** Verbatim migration prevents content loss but can accidentally publish stale or unsupported business claims as searchable facts.

**How to apply:** Bind approval to the exact reviewed revision everywhere searchable text can appear: detail metadata, listing and preview cards, related links, and crawler artifacts. A summary payload cannot prove full-revision approval; use a complete revision identity. Canonicalize dates before hashing so archival strings, database dates, and API ISO timestamps agree. Show an archival fallback only after an authoritative lookup confirms no CMS counterpart exists. Missing, pending, failed, or changed resolutions stay off indexable surfaces. Global indexing gates remain independent and may be stricter.

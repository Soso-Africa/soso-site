---
name: Video-only hero artwork
description: Owner requirement preventing unrelated product imagery from flashing before hero playback.
---

Video-mode homepage heroes must never display the separate image-mode artwork, including during loading, playback failure, reduced motion, or data saving.

**Why:** The business owner explicitly rejected the unrelated black-kaftan flash before the uploaded atelier video.

**How to apply:** Keep image-mode artwork separate from video playback. Use a neutral background when video cannot display; any future poster must be derived from the actual selected video, not an unrelated product image.

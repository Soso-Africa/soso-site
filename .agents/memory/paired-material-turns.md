---
name: Paired material turns
description: Truthful rules for scroll-driven front/back product imagery and fabric-motion expectations.
---

Treat each material or colourway as an ordered pair of genuine front and back photographs. Drive the product experience from normal page-scroll progress through each pair before advancing to the next material.

**Why:** A flat image carousel does not communicate the garment’s silhouette, while unrelated gallery images cannot safely be inferred as front/back pairs.

**How to apply:** Staff must explicitly name and upload both views for each set. Keep legacy gallery images separate, preserve material ordering, and provide direct Front/Back and material controls alongside scrolling.

Two stills support only a restrained turn illusion: rotate each photograph toward an edge, crossfade before 90 degrees, and use subtle depth and sway. Do not claim true 3D reconstruction or physically accurate localized fabric movement without intermediate frames, video, or a verified geometry/mask workflow.

**Why:** Inventing hidden angles or aggressive deformation can mirror faces, distort bodies, and misrepresent garment construction.

**How to apply:** Keep rotation below edge-on, honor reduced motion, never trap page scrolling, and leave purchase controls in normal reachable document flow.

---
name: Purpose-limited launch interest
description: Consent boundary for shopper requests to hear about unavailable accessory launches.
---

Accessory launch-interest requests are an essential, shopper-initiated service and must not depend on optional analytics or marketing-cookie consent. Require an explicit unchecked consent for this narrow email purpose, and retain the applicable wording/policy version with the request.

**Why:** A shopper may request a product-specific launch email while declining optional tracking. Keeping those choices separate avoids coercive consent, while versioning preserves what the shopper agreed to.

**How to apply:** Use this boundary for product-specific availability alerts. Do not broaden the request into general marketing permission, and never send the submitted email or free-form data into analytics.

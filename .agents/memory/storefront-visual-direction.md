---
name: Approved storefront visual direction
description: Client-approved visual and conversion direction for SOSO public shopping surfaces.
---

Keep the public SOSO storefront bright, white-led, spacious and commercially clear, using Hobbs-style retail restraint as a reference without copying its brand. Preserve the dark palette only for the private Staff workspace.

**Why:** The previous dark editorial treatment was explicitly rejected as too dark and insufficiently shoppable.

**How to apply:** Extend the bright semantic tokens across every public route, dialog and purchase state. Keep paid checkout as the dominant action, stylist help secondary and in-app, and do not restore a global WhatsApp-order control or hero slogan.

---
name: Approved storefront visual direction
description: Client-approved visual and conversion direction for SOSO public shopping surfaces.
---

Keep SOSO bright, white-led, spacious and commercially clear, using Hobbs-style retail restraint as a reference without copying its brand. At the homepage top, the menu overlays the hero transparently, then becomes a persistent white header as the shopper scrolls. Use the black SOSO wordmark on light surfaces and white over hero media. Staff sign-in, owner setup, and the private Staff workspace also use clean white or warm-white surfaces with dark text and restrained accents.

**Why:** The previous dark editorial treatment was explicitly rejected as too dark and insufficiently shoppable. Later corrections extended the white-led direction to Staff authentication and workspace surfaces, while retaining administrative clarity through structure rather than a dark palette.

**How to apply:** Extend bright semantic tokens across every public route, dialog and purchase state. The homepage hero shows only its primary shopping CTA over the media—no slogan, eyebrow, description, stylist CTA, or assurances. Lead with product categories immediately afterward, alternate rich two- and four-column product modules, and place service assurances later. Keep paid checkout dominant, stylist help secondary and in-app, and do not restore a global WhatsApp-order control.

The five homepage category panels use approved rotating editorial imagery: each combines the original SOSO catalogue photo with both black and ivory AI campaign directions, crossfading only while visible. Keep timing staggered, respect reduced-motion preferences, and leave image sets, mode, crop, and timing editable in Staff.

**Why:** The merchant approved both generated directions for every category and explicitly requested the dynamic multi-photo behavior as a core part of the homepage merchandising.

Category transitions must feel continuous rather than like slideshow swaps: keep frames mounted and overlapping, use a slow opacity crossfade with a restrained scale/swivel, and never expose the light page background between images.

**Why:** A simple stacked swap produced a crude light flash and was explicitly rejected.

The announcement strip is campaign space, not permanent page furniture. Staff must be able to add, reorder, or remove every announcement; when the list is empty, render no strip and leave no gap.

**Why:** The merchant clarified that promotions and discounts are temporary Hobbs-like campaigns and that the default homepage should not display evergreen promotional filler.

**How to apply:** Ship with no announcement items. Preserve merchant-authored campaigns during schema upgrades, but remove only the known shipped defaults. Keep the Staff editor usable when the list is empty.

---
name: Conversion review evidence
description: How to make reliable conversion audits of live storefronts
---

When auditing a live storefront, verify each claim at three levels: the purchase block, the full page below the fold, and sitewide navigation/footer destinations. Report “missing” only when the content is absent at all relevant levels; otherwise distinguish “present but buried,” “not product-specific,” and “not connected to the buying decision.”

**Why:** A size chart and policy links can exist in page source or below the fold while still being hard for a buyer to discover. Treating those as absent overstates the diagnosis and weakens trust in the rest of the review.

**How to apply:** Re-check the exact product URL, inspect the complete rendered text and page source, capture desktop/mobile views where possible, and label confidence/limitations for performance measurements and dynamic interactions.

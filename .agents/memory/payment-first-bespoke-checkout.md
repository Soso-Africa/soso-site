---
name: Payment-first bespoke checkout
description: SOSO’s required order of operations for its made-to-order purchase journey.
---

SOSO’s bespoke checkout must not insert an atelier or production-confirmation gate before payment. Customers select their piece and size, then pay; the atelier confirms making details and production next steps afterwards. Stylist contact is an optional enquiry route for shoppers who want help before paying. Custom sizing remains visible whenever a menswear product is custom-eligible, even if commerce mapping is not ready; only checkout stays disabled until the Custom variant is safely mapped.

**Why:** The business prioritizes a quick payment path. Pre-payment confirmation introduces unnecessary friction for shoppers who are ready to buy.

**How to apply:** Keep primary cart and checkout calls to action payment-oriented. Position WhatsApp or stylist assistance as a secondary, optional path. Do not use missing commerce mappings to hide custom-size discovery. Future payment and JusticeSure integrations must preserve the payment-first sequence.
